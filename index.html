About Us

Gasa and Winny's Poultry Farm is a small growing company based in Umzimkhulu township in a village called Mfulamhle.

This is a partnership company founded by Lwazi Msomi (Gasa) and Bawinile Tshabalala (Winny).

We focus on growing and supplying chickens nationwide.

Objectives

To give healthy and fresh meat at the lowest price. We also focus on supplying chickens to small growing businesses and large companies as per the needs of the companies.

How It Works

Customers can visit our site and buy using their own transportation. We also do deliveries to companies or people who can't come to the site. For placing an order, contact or WhatsApp any number provided on our site.
